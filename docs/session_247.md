# Session 247 — Session 115 narration

The session that found nothing was the session that proved the
process.

It opened with a reset workspace and the s113 routine: clone, copy the
example env, push the schema, plant the seed, count the rows. The
census matched on the first try. The Playwright cache was missing one
headless shell — 1243, wanted by the 1.63 toolchain, absent from the
cache the reset left behind — and then the gate ran green at exactly
the numbers the s114 ship had promised: one hundred ninety-four
hundred and forty-eight tests, one hundred thirty-seven e2e checks,
zero flakes. Both audits clean, every ordinal standing, the reference
bundle the same md5 it has been for eighty-six consecutive probes.

The rotation list had three names on it, and the first thing the scout
did was cross one of them off — not because it was a bad candidate,
but because it was not actually a maiden. The settings Defaults tab
was listed as un-walked, and the list was wrong: session 81 had walked
it, thirty-four sessions back, and its suite header said so plainly to
anyone who read the plans instead of the narration. That is a lesson
worth the price of the walk: the session-close narrations are memory,
the plans are ledger, and the ledger is what you check.

So the Defaults walk became a re-verification, and a re-verification
that found nothing is not a wasted walk. It measured, for the first
time since the compensator was written, that our v4 margin trick
really does compute the reference's v3 gaps: twelve pixels from label
to control, eight of margin, on both apps, at both widths. It measured
the select dropdown and its two options byte-for-byte. It measured the
choose-and-save round-trip, ours one PUT, theirs a four-request entity
dance, both landing the same state. And it found the one thing on the
surface that was ours and not theirs: our six labels are wired to
their inputs, and theirs are not. The reference ships six bare labels
that point at nothing — its own accessibility defect, the same genus
as the unnamed camera button and the unassociated Danger confirm.
Ours stays. Ours is now pinned.

The second family could not be walked on the reference at all. Its
demo data has been zero since session three; its accounts table is one
empty-state row; the insights dialog cannot open. So the walk went
ours-only, against the contract the bundle decoded, the way the row
menus went in s113 — and the dialog, opened live for the first time in
its hundred-and-fifteen-session life, turned out to be exactly what
the decode said it was: the stock chrome, the green badge, the three
stat cards at two hundred twenty-nine pixels, the segmented tabs, the
tinted icon boxes, the avatar circles, the initials formula verbatim.
Nothing to fix. Everything to freeze — because nothing on that
surface had ever been pinned live, and an unpinned parity is a parity
that can drift.

The freezes are the session. Two unit pins, two e2e batteries, all
added inside existing files and existing tests, the census untouched
at one thirty-seven. The RED run failed fourteen times, every failure
a document that had not yet been told the new count; the stash proof
gave the fourteen back after the native eighty-five. GREEN caught one
more — the line-census pin doing exactly what it was designed to do,
reading the source, counting the lines, and finding the test file had
grown. Both new pins were proven reversible by name: drop an htmlFor,
fail the association pin; flip the variant, fail the variant pin;
restore both, green.

The e2e phase taught its own small lessons, the way it always does.
Playwright's getByText matches substrings, and "Manufacturing" is a
substring of "Al Noor Manufacturing" — strict mode caught it in one
run. "Open Deals" turned out to name three things at once, a label, a
tab, and an empty state — caught in the next. Exact matches and a
class-scoped locator, and the suite passed one thirty-seven of one
thirty-seven with the new batteries green inside it.

And the captures closed it the way they always do — a reader with no
code reading all six labels and the four-and-two split off the pixels,
the exact stat values, the three tabs, the containment — with one
adjudication that was really a confession: the first phone capture
showed "Week" where "Month" belonged, because the walk script's
restore had been a silent catch, and the throwaway database kept the
state the script thought it had put back. The setting was restored
through the seam, the capture retaken, the second read confirmed
Month. The e2e freeze, whose restore asserts its response, had been
right all along.

Session 115 shipped at ee094b1, pushed and remote-verified, main only,
keys shredded. The count ladder: one hundred nine files, one thousand
nine hundred fifty unit tests, one hundred thirty-seven e2e checks,
badge two thousand eighty-seven. Zero source changes. The Defaults
family re-verified after thirty-four sessions, the insights dialog
walked and frozen, and the un-walked list down to one name: the
reports tables. Session 116 knows where to look.
